## What this project is

I'm building and testing software that helps voice assistants on phone calls know **when a
person has finished speaking**, when they are just saying "haan/hmm" while listening, and
when they want the assistant to wait ("ek minute"). To do that I need recordings of real
people talking to each other in Hindi, English and Hinglish.

## What you'll do

- Have one or two conversations of about 20–25 minutes with another participant over a voice
  call (Discord, Zoom or a phone call), playing roles from printed cards: one person plays a
  customer-care agent, the other a customer. Then you swap roles.
- Some numbers on the cards (phone numbers, account numbers, amounts) are **made up**. Please
  **never say your real phone number, Aadhaar, PAN, card or bank details** during the recording.
- The recording should take about **1 hour** in total.

## What is recorded and kept

- Your voice, on its own audio track.
- A transcript produced automatically from the audio.
- Basic details you give me: age range (18+ only), gender, home state/region, and which
  languages you speak. **No name is stored with the recordings**; you get a speaker ID like `SPK017`.
- What device you used (phone handset, earphones, speakerphone).

## Who can access it and where it's stored

- The recordings are stored on my own computers. They may also be stored in private cloud
  storage used for training models (for example a private Kaggle dataset), which only I can access.
- The calls pass through the calling service you use (Discord, Zoom or a telephony provider),
  under that service's own terms.
- [Delete this line if not used] Transcripts may be produced by Sarvam AI's speech-recognition
  service, which processes the audio on its servers.

## How it will be used

- To train and test turn-taking models, and to build a **test benchmark**.
- Results (numbers, charts and short anonymised examples) may appear in a public write-up,
  a GitHub repository or a blog post.

## Public release (optional, a separate choice)

I'd like to publish part of the benchmark so others can test their systems.

- **Labels and timings** (when each person spoke, with no audio) will be published for everyone.
- **Your audio** will be published **only if you tick the box below**. If you don't tick it,
  your audio stays private, and you can still take part.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop the recording at any time.
- You can **withdraw later**: message me with your speaker ID and I will delete your
  recordings and stop using them. Anything already published as part of aggregate results
  (overall numbers) can't be un-published, but your audio will be removed from any future
  release and from anything I control.
- Recordings will be deleted by **[date, e.g. 31 Dec 2027]** unless you agreed to public release.
