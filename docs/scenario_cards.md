# Scenario cards

Print one **Agent** card and one **Customer** card per scenario. Take every number from the
session's fake-PII card (`fake_pii_cards/session_NNN.md`), **never real ones**. Speak naturally
in your normal Hindi/English mix, and don't read the cards word for word.

**Every customer, every scenario:**
- ⏸ Ask the agent to **wait** at least twice, then actually go quiet for a few seconds
  ("ek minute…", "ruko zara", "hold karo, dekh ke batata/batati hoon").
- ✋ **Interrupt** the agent at least twice, during the agent's long explanation.
- 🔢 Read numbers **in groups with pauses** ("nau-aath-saat… do-teen… …").
- 🗣 Say "haan" sometimes as **a real answer** ("haan, wahi number hai") and sometimes just to show you're listening.
- 💬 Pause mid-sentence before the verb sometimes, like you would naturally ("main kal… payment… kar dunga/dungi").

**Every agent, every scenario:**
- Give at least one **long explanation** (charges, process, terms) of 20–30 s.
- Use natural "haan ji", "achha", "hmm", "theek hai" while the customer is talking.
- Ask the customer to read out at least two numbers.

---

## 1. Loan EMI reminder (collections)
**Agent:** You're calling from a finance company about an EMI that's **overdue**.
Confirm identity (loan account number, date of birth). State the overdue amount and late fee. Ask
*when* they'll pay. **Long explanation:** late-fee rules, credit-score impact, payment options (UPI, net banking, branch).
Get a promise-to-pay date and amount.
**Customer:** You know the EMI is late. You'll pay, but not today. Ask for time. Argue about the late fee once.
Go and check your banking app ("ek minute, app khol raha/rahi hoon") before confirming the amount.

## 2. KYC re-verification
**Agent:** Bank KYC is expiring. Verify name, DOB, Aadhaar-format number, PAN-format number and address
with PIN code. **Long explanation:** why re-KYC is needed and what happens if it's not done.
**Customer:** You need to **fetch your documents** ("ruko, Aadhaar card dhoondh raha/rahi hoon") and come back.
Read the numbers slowly in groups. Get one digit wrong, then correct it.

## 3. Delivery address confirmation
**Agent:** An order (order ID on the card) can't be delivered. Confirm the full address, landmark, PIN code and an
alternate number. Offer a delivery slot. **Long explanation:** the reschedule policy and the return-to-origin rule.
**Customer:** The address is complicated (flat, tower, society, landmark "mandir ke paas"). Change your mind about the slot once.

## 4. Lost debit card: block request
**Agent:** The customer called to block a card. Verify the card number, last transaction and amount.
Block the card and explain the reissue. **Long explanation:** card-reissue charges, delivery time, and the dispute process.
**Customer:** You're a bit worried. Interrupt to ask "paisa wapas milega na?" Read the card number in 4-digit groups.
Check your SMS for the last transaction ("ek second, message dekhta/dekhti hoon").

## 5. Clinic appointment booking
**Agent:** Book a doctor's appointment. Collect the patient's name, age, phone number, preferred date and time, and symptoms in brief.
**Long explanation:** the clinic timings, consultation fee, and documents to bring.
**Customer:** You're booking for a parent. Check with them in the middle ("ek minute, mummy se pooch ke batata/batati hoon").
Change the time once.

## 6. Insurance renewal
**Agent:** A policy (policy number on the card) is due for renewal. Confirm the nominee and premium amount, and
offer an add-on. **Long explanation:** the add-on's benefits and the grace period.
**Customer:** You're not sure you want the add-on. Ask the agent to repeat the premium. Dictate the amount back.

## 7. Mobile recharge complaint
**Agent:** The customer says a recharge failed but the money was deducted. Collect the mobile number, recharge amount,
transaction ID and date. **Long explanation:** the refund timeline and escalation steps.
**Customer:** You're irritated. Interrupt more than usual. Read the transaction ID slowly (letters and numbers).

## 8. Free chat (5 min, no card)
No roles. Just talk: weekend plans, a film or series, cricket, food, college or work. Overlap, laugh, interrupt and
backchannel naturally. This is the most natural turn-taking data in the session.
